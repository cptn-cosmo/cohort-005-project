# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys courses and works through their lessons.
_Avoid_: Student (except as the name of the role), user, customer

**Admin**:
Staff member who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask, with a written reason, for their money back on one purchase. An Admin either Approves or Declines it. A purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one pending at once, and none after it has been Refunded.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation

**Refund**:
The money given back to the Learner once a Refund Request is Approved: an amount the Admin sets, up to what the Learner actually paid for the purchase. A purchase has at most one Refund, and any Refund, partial or full, ends access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, outside the platform. It is not a Refund Request and must never be used to name one.
